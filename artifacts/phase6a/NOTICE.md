# Phase 6A artifact notice

`summary.json` contains compact results derived from the ETH ASL Change
Detection Office dataset. It does not contain or redistribute dataset images,
ROS bags, meshes, or point clouds.

Dataset and method:

- Marius Fehr, Fadri Furrer, Ivan Dryanovski, Jurgen Sturm, Igor
  Gilitschenski, Roland Siegwart, and Cesar Cadena. “TSDF-based Change
  Detection for Consistent Long-Term Dense Reconstruction and Dynamic Object
  Discovery.” *IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation*,
  2017.
- [ETH dataset page](https://projects.asl.ethz.ch/datasets/change-detection/)
- [Paper](https://cesarcadena.ethz.ch/files/ICRA2017_mfehr.pdf)

The VLM counts describe a cached structured review of geometric candidates.
They are not human-labelled precision, recall, F1, or accuracy measurements.
